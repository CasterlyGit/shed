shed. Claude Code that learns you.

A silent shadow layer for Claude Code. Picks the 2-3 most relevant notes from your past and prepends them to every prompt. Watches for corrections, queues them as proposed lessons. Grooms its own memory. Local embeddings, no LLM call, <200ms. Local-only by default, every proposal manually approved.
